Our story

From a 12-person fit-out team to a Grade 1 contractor.

Meridian started with small office fit-outs in Heliopolis. Clients kept us for one reason: the systems we installed worked when the building opened. That reputation brought hospitals, then towers, then resorts on the Red Sea.

Today we are a turnkey electromechanical contractor with 650 engineers and technicians, an in-house design office and a prefabrication workshop — but the rule is the same as in 2009: a building is not finished until its systems are commissioned and its operators are trained.

Milestones

Sixteen years, one building at a time.

  1. 2009

    Founded in Heliopolis as an office fit-out MEP contractor.

  2. 2013

    First hospital project; classified as a Grade 1 electromechanical contractor.

  3. 2016

    Licensed fire-protection division launched with Civil Defence approvals.

  4. 2019

    Opened the 10th of Ramadan prefabrication workshop; ISO 45001 certification.

  5. 2022

    Red Sea branch opened to serve hospitality projects on the coast.

  6. 2025

    140th project handed over; first Tier III data centre under construction.
