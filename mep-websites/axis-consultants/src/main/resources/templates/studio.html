Axis was founded in 2013 by two engineers who had spent a decade fixing on site what should have been solved on paper. Today the studio is 38 people in Dokki — mechanical, electrical, public-health, fire and ELV engineers, BIM specialists and energy modellers — working on buildings across Egypt and the Gulf.

We are independent: we do not install, supply or represent any manufacturer. Our only product is the design, so our only incentive is that it works.

Principles

Three things we won’t compromise on.

  1. Coordinated, not just drawn.

    Every project is modelled and clash-checked across all disciplines before it is issued for tender.

  2. Performance you can measure.

    We set energy, comfort and water targets at concept and report against them at every stage.

  3. Buildable and maintainable.

    Plant rooms with access, risers with spare capacity and specifications the local market can actually supply.
