How the estimate works

  1. Area bands. The first 10,000 m² is priced in full, the next 40,000 m² at 85% and anything above 50,000 m² at 70%.
  2. Discipline rates. Each discipline has its own rate per m², reflecting design effort.
  3. Complexity. Hospitals, labs and data centres take more engineering per m² than offices.
  4. Add-ons. BIM coordination and site supervision are priced as a share of the design fee.
